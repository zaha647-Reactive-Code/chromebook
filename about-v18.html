Who we are

As a venture of Tech Valley — an official Google for Education and ChromeOS Partner in Pakistan — MyChromebook.pk is dedicated to empowering education through technology.

We launched this platform to address the need for genuine, high-quality Google Chromebooks in a market often flooded with unreliable, grey-market devices. Our commitment is to provide only authentic products that ensure performance, reliability, and peace of mind.

Whether you're a student, teacher, or organization, our mission is to support your digital journey with the right tools for learning and productivity.
